    Priority list

    All 731 species with their Barcoding Priority Score (BPS) and its components. Sort by any column, filter with the fields under the column names, and download the rows you see.

    Column definitions
    Global and local category
    Priority category from the global perspective (IUCN Red List, global mammal phylogeny) and the local perspective (SALVE national red list, Brazilian mammal phylogeny). Highest and near priority species are threatened; research priority species are Data Deficient or unassessed; watch priority species are Least Concern or Near Threatened.
    BPS
    Barcoding Priority Score: evolutionary distinctiveness (ED₂) × extinction probability × probability of omission from reference databases (PO). Median across posterior draws and trees.
    ED₂
    Evolutionary distinctiveness, in million years, on the global or Brazilian phylogeny.
    GE₂ and LE₂
    Probability of extinction over 50 years, from the IUCN Red List (GE₂) or SALVE (LE₂).
    PO
    Modelled probability that the species lacks a reference sequence, across the 10 primers.
    Primers with sequences
    Number of the 10 primers for which the species has at least one reference sequence.

    Reference sequences

    Download the reference sequences of Brazilian mammals used in the paper, for one or more primers, filtered by order, priority category or location. Sequences were extracted from MIDORI2 (GenBank release 264) with CRABS.

    About

    This site accompanies Luypaert, Farias, Hrbek, Peres & Haugaasen (2026), Knowledge gaps and sequencing priorities for mammalian eDNA metabarcoding in the world’s largest tropical forest country, Nature Communications. The priority lists correspond to Tables S4 and S5, and the maps to Fig. 5.

    Range maps are from Marsh et al. (2022), so the 48 species without a range map, mostly recently described or split rodents and primates, are listed in the table but not on the map.

    Common names are from the Mammal Diversity Database. Red List categories are from the IUCN Red List (version 2025-2) and SALVE.
